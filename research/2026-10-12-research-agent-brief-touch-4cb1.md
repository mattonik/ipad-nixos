## What happened on hardware (read `research/2026-10-11-touch-upload-hardware-result.md`, the 2026-10-12 section, and `research/2026-10-11-touch-no-ack-analysis.md`)
With Apple's recovered setup (LSB-first, mode 0, Apple's power order, wake `19 c1`, detect frame passing with `1f 01`), we send the constructed firmware packet (59,288 bytes, starts `18 e1`, produced offline by `boot/touch_fw_packets.py` from the J81 `Constructed Firmware` property) as ONE transmit-only SPI transfer (the transfer really completes: 103 ms at the measured clock), then the 2-byte query `1a a1`. The reply is **`4c b1`** on all five packet+query attempts, identically for word delays 0/5 us and requested SPI clocks 0.5, 1.984 and 4 MHz (the real clock is ~2.3x the requested one). Apple requires `4b c1`. Before the transfer, any frame returned the idle word `1f 01`.
Observation: Apple's known status words `4a d1` (register write OK), `4b c1` (data OK) and now `4c b1` all have the form `0x40|n, (0x17-n)<<4|1` (n = 0xa, 0xb, 0xc), i.e. a status code with a complement.

## Questions (static analysis of `AppleMultitouchSPI`, `MTSPIBootloader_Z2`/`_N1`, the HBPP code)
1. **Status words.** List every status word the driver compares or logs after ATN/ACK queries (`4ad1`, `4bc1`, `4cb1`, `4879`, `4969`, `1f01`, `18e1`, `1aa1`, others), with the code (n) meaning of each if the driver or its strings name it. What does `4cb1` (n = 0xc) mean: checksum/length error, a "busy", "wait for the next packet", "firmware accepted but needs more", something else? Cite function addresses and strings (E = read in code, I = inference, U = unknown).
2. **Packet framing the ROM validates.** What does the ROM check in a data packet: the `18 e1` header, the 12-byte internal header fields (length, address, flags), the trailing 4-byte sum, alignment, maximum length? How is the Constructed Firmware property laid out compared with what `MTSPIBootloader` sends, and does the driver add or change anything (a length-prefix, byte order of the 16-bit words, a pre-command that sets the load address or size) before the transfer? Specifically: does Apple send a small command (e.g. an address/size setup, an erase or "prepare for download" frame) BEFORE the firmware transfer that we are missing, so that the ROM answers `4b c1` instead of `4c b1`?
3. **What `4cb1` after a single big transfer implies.** Is there evidence the ROM expects the firmware in several chunks (the unconstructed path uses at most 0x3f0-byte payloads) even for the constructed property, or that the constructed property is supposed to be sent through the DMA path with a different length rounding? Check the retry logic: when the status is not `4bc1` the driver resends the whole chunk; is there any other branch (e.g. a status that means "continue", "resend the last chunk", or "busy: poll again")?
4. **State the ROM is in.** After the failed attempt, would the ROM still answer the detect frame, and is there a documented way to recover or restart a download (reset, a specific command)?
