## What I verified myself
- **The setter exists and is as described (T1).** Disassembly of 0x6c8a0c4 in the 19H394 kernel: `w21 = 0xfffde000; w22 = (w1 != 0) ? 0x80000000 : 0; w20 = w21 + 0x44000 (= 0x22000); w23 = w21 + 0x44171`; per iteration `delta = w20 + w21`, skip if `delta == 0x120` or `(delta - 0x21) < 0x5f`; read old (helper at 0x6c8a124), write `(old & 0x7fffffff) | w22` (0x6c8a13c), step +0x10 while `w20 < w23`. That visits 0x22000..0x22180 on the 16-byte grid except 0x22030..0x22070 and 0x22120: 19 words. The 32-bit wrap explains why no literal 0x22000 exists in the code.
- **The store capture contains the requests.** `j81-ios-pmgr-store-slice` and `...power-store-sample` have `00022000=80000000`, `00022090=89070300`, `000220a0=89040300`, `000220b0=89060300`, `00022130=89040300` and 14 more (correcting my earlier search, which only looked at the third capture).
- **Linux values** (fresh boot 83536afb, `linux-pmgr-0x22000-0x223fc-words.txt`): the same 19 words have bit 31 set on Linux in 14 cases; the five that do not are +0x22000 (0x00000000), +0x22090 (0x09070300), +0x220a0 (0x09040300), +0x220b0 (0x09060300), +0x22130 (0x09040300). +0x22120 (0x09040300) also lacks bit 31 but the loop skips it, and +0x22030..0x22070 are 0 and skipped.
- **Ordering.** The loop is called from the parent 0x6bccefc (slot +0x688, enable=1) after the J81 slot +0x5c8 method (0x6c8872c, the page-1/page-4 update); the stock audit puts MiscACG (+0x690) and the voltage-manager enable (+0x698) after it, and SoC TVM later in the feature loop. So Apple's order is PLL1, PLL4, MCC bank, MiscACG, voltage manager, TVM. Our earlier Linux tests used MiscACG -> TVM -> PLL1 -> PLL4, the reverse of that for the first group.
