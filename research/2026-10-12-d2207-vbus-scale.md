# D2207 VBUS ADC scale confirmed from the J81 code

**Date:** 2026-10-12. Static analysis only (Ghidra decompile of the iPad5,3 12B410 `__PRELINK_TEXT`, base `0xffffff8002593000`). No hardware, no downloads.
Parent note: `research/2026-10-11-d2207-vbus-adc.md` (its "Conversion formulas" table is wrong in its units, see below).

Evidence labels: E = read in the code, I = inferred, U = unknown.

## Result

The J81 conversion routines do not return mV or mA. They return **16.16 fixed-point** volts and amps. The 88 and 52.8 factors are correct, but the unit is 1/65536 of a volt or amp, not 1 mV or 1 mA. The hardware-observed "divide by about 64" is therefore really a divide by **65536/1000 = 65.536** (a shift by 16 after the `/1000`), not a shift by 6. The two differ by 2.4 %.

## Formulas (E unless noted)

Sample, `readADCGated` `0xffffff8002b4821c`: `raw = (byte1 << 4) | (byte0 & 0x0f)`, 12 bits, stored unchanged (E). For channel 9 it returns an error if bit 7 of byte0 is set (E). No shift, no extra fractional bits.

Conversions (vtable slots `0x5d8` voltage at `0xffffff8002b49200`, `0x5d0` current at `0xffffff8002b494c0`; both call `readADCGated` through slot `0x608` with flags 0):

| Quantity | Channel | Result of the conversion routine (16.16) | Real unit |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| VBUS voltage | 19 | `out = (raw * 88000) / 1000 = raw * 88` (jump-table entry for 0x13 loads 88000, `0xffffff8002b49324`; shared signed divide by 1000 via multiply by `0x20c49ba5e353f7cf`) | volts = `out / 65536` |
| VBUS current | 9 | `out = (raw * 0xce40) / 1000 = floor(raw * 52800 / 1000)` | amps = `out / 65536` |

Wrappers `0xffffff8002b4cbdc` (ch 19) and `0xffffff8002b4cbf4` (ch 9) call helpers `0xffffff8002b41aa4` / `0xffffff8002b41b20`, which compute `(out * 0x10000 + 0x8000) >> 16`. With the multiplier `0x10000` this is the identity, so the wrapper result is still 16.16 volts/amps.

Why 16.16 and not mV (E for the arithmetic, I for the conclusion):
- Channels 0/4 (battery-type) compute `raw * 40000 / 1000 + 0x9c40000/1000`, i.e. `2.5 * 65536 + raw * 40`: 2.5 V offset plus 12 bits of 40/65536 V, spanning 2.5 to 5.0 V. Only 16.16 volts fits.
- Full scales are round numbers only in 16.16: channel 19 gives 4095 * 88 / 65536 = 5.50 V, channel 9 gives 4095 * 52.8 / 65536 = 3.30 A (3.3 A is the reference maximum input current). With /64 they would be 5.6 V and 3.4 A.
- The power-source code that clamps the input limit (`0xffffff8002b4c610`) does its arithmetic in 16.16 (`>> 0x10`, constants such as `0x50000` = 5.0, `0x3e80000` = 1000.0).

## Constants for patch 0099

```text
voltage: out = raw * 88;              microvolts = out * 15625 / 1024   (= raw * 1342.77 uV)
current: out = raw * 52800 / 1000;    microamps  = out * 15625 / 1024   (= raw * 805.66 uA)
```

(`15625/1024 = 1e6/65536`.) Use 64-bit arithmetic. Check against the hardware: raw 3541 -> 4.755 V; raw 591 -> 476 mA (the observed 476-484 mA flat run, 4.87 V was the /64 approximation); raw 577 -> 465 mA.

## Open (U)

- I did not trace the `AppleD2207PMUPowerSource` property that publishes input current in mA, so I cannot say whether iOS divides by 65536 and scales to mA there. The 16.16 reading rests on the conversion arithmetic above, not on that caller.
- No independent meter calibration; a USB meter would still be the only absolute check of the 5.5 V / 3.3 A full scales.

## One-line recommendation

In patch 0099 replace the 88000 uV and 52800 uA per count by: `uV = raw*88*15625/1024` and `uA = (raw*52800/1000)*15625/1024`.

## Hardware validation of the 16.16 conversion (T7 payload, 2026-10-12, user-approved)

Bounded read on the corrected kernel: control bytes `0x0500`/`0x0501`/`0x007b` = `0x00`/`0x00`/`0xfe` before, after each of four conversions, and at the end (exact restore), no timeout, no kernel error. voltage_now 4,256,591 and 4,279,418 uV; current_now 1,027,221 and 1,036,071 uA. Plausible USB values; the voltage (4.26-4.28 V) is below the 4.4 V acceptance guide because the source sags under a ~1.03 A load (the PMIC's USB-power-limited bit is set).
