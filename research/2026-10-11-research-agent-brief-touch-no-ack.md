## What happened on hardware (read `research/2026-10-11-touch-upload-hardware-result.md`)
Power order, reset release and the boot IRQ are all as Apple does. We then sent the 59,288-byte constructed firmware packet (`18 e1 ...`) as one SPI message (mode 2, 1.984 MHz) and the `1a a1` query. The reply is always `f0 01` (the idle pattern the ROM returns to every frame), expected `4b c1`. Repeating the query for ~1.3 s, 500 kHz, and chunking (CS held) changed nothing. The first frame we send after reset release is an all-zero-reply "dummy"; we send NO wake frame before the packet in upload mode.
