## Reading
Only +0x22000 differs in the neighbourhood: +0x22004, +0x22008, +0x2200c, +0x22010 and +0x22014 are identical on iPadOS and Linux. So the difference is the single word +0x22000, not a four-register group that the kernel's record API fills (the hypothesis in `2026-11-20-j81-22000-stock-lookup.md` for the 0x6bcf280 record writer is not supported: its other registers, if they are +0x22004..+0x2200c, are 0 on iPadOS too). The stock setter is still unlocated; the bootloader clears this bit, and iPadOS has it set before the GPC request.
