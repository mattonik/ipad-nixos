# CPU TVM shares the J81 DPE resource

The J81 19H394 PMGR tree sets `cpu-tvm=1`. Its primary startup call requests a 64-bit read/modify/write at **0x202220048**. The expected enabled value is the old register value with bit 0 set. An archived CPU-cluster dump that covers DPE may also cover this word. SoC TVM uses a different resource and access width at 0x20e02d000.

## Stock enable path

Addresses below are kernel VA suffixes. The audit in `tools/audit_j81_pmgr_start.py` checks the stock kernel and device-tree digests and the relevant dispatch and instruction words.

1. Descriptor 2 dispatches at 0x6bccd34 through slot +0x6a8 with rail 0, effective enable Boolean, wait argument 1 and instance 0. After this returns, a separate conditional at 0x6bccd5c can dispatch rail 7 if object+0x19c38 is at least 2. This analysis covers the first call.
2. The J81 wrapper at 0x6c88df0 takes the rail-zero branch. It optionally invokes a callback through the pointer at object+0x35038, passing a pointer to a word containing 1 before the parent and 0 afterward. Its side effects depend on the installed callback.
3. On enable, 0x6c88e78 branches around the +0x50 bit-31 polls directly to the parent call. The waits in that wrapper belong to the disable-with-wait branch. The parent at 0x6bcf590 selects rail 0, checks the instance count and mapping, reads selector 8 offset 0x48 through 0x6bcf7f0, replaces bit 0, and tail-calls the 64-bit writer at 0x6bcf858. The parent contains no enable-completion poll on this branch.

The direct operation supplies a CPU-side control request. It cannot establish GPU fabric readiness. A bit-0 difference across matched boots would justify following the callback and its timing. A match across boots with different GPU-ID outcomes would lower the priority of that bit as the discriminator. Neither observation alone establishes causality.

A case-insensitive literal-address scan of the archived `artifacts/live/gpu` tree's .log and .txt files under 2 MiB found no 0x202220048 or DPE word-base address (with or without the 0x prefix). Binary ranges, offset-labelled dumps and other archive trees remain open. For the next comparison, retain capture width, base, timestamp or boot stage, and the GPU-ID outcome alongside each word.
